## Phase Goal

**As a** shop owner, **I want to** have stock reserved by a single guaranteed-atomic operation, **so that** the quantity I open for sale can never be oversold.

<objective>
Prove the phase's defining risk — the oversell-safe guarded atomic decrement — at the service level against a real PostgreSQL 17, and lay the complete commerce schema that every later slice builds on. This plan delivers the guaranteed-correct `reserve()`/`release()` primitive plus the pure pricing and order-status logic that the `POST /orders` slice (01-02) and box slice (01-05) consume unchanged.

Purpose: The core value of Saladee is "จำนวนที่เปิดขายตรงกับผลผลิตจริงเสมอ — ไม่ oversell." Correctness is a database property (guarded single-statement UPDATE), and it must be proven with a real race before any endpoint is layered on top.
Output: `schema.ts` (12 tables + 5 enums), reversible `0001_commerce` migration pair applied to the DB, `services/{reservation,pricing,order-status}.ts`, and passing unit + real-PG concurrency tests.
</objective>

<planning_decisions>
Decisions this plan LOCKS (resolving RESEARCH Open Questions):
- **OQ-1 (cancel from shipping):** `shipping → cancelled` is **FORBIDDEN**. `TRANSITIONS.shipping = ["done"]`. Rationale: plants have left the farm and are not resellable in-round; forbidding removes the release-ambiguity branch. Returns are a Phase-2+ concern.
- **OQ-2 (daily price override):** nullable `effective_date` column on `prices` + `UNIQUE(round_id, variety_id, tier, effective_date)`. NULL = round default; a dated row wins for that day. No separate table.
- **OQ-3 (products vs varieties):** **No `products` table.** The sellable surface is `varieties` (each carries name/category/description/image + its own `sale_units`) plus `boxes`. Sale mode (pre-order vs ready-to-ship, SALE-04) is derived from round timing (D-11), not stored. INV-01 "manage products" is satisfied by variety CRUD (01-03).
</planning_decisions>
