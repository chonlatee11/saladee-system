Close UAT Gap 1 (test 2, major — "ไม่เห็นรูปภาพแสดงเลย"). Purpose: A web customer sees real product photos on the catalog — the storefront's first-impression surface.
Output: catalog coverUrl field + card/product-page consumption + placeholder + R2 config doc; UAT re-check.
</objective>
