Add the idempotent QR re-fetch.

Purpose: Cover ORD-01 (order lands), PAY-01 (QR), the creation half of PAY-03 (hold scheduled), DEL-04 (delivery at checkout), and PLAT-04 (consent logging).

</objective>

## Trust Boundaries
| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| LIFF client → POST /orders | Untrusted cart, delivery choice, price, consent cross here. |
| API → pg-boss | Hold-expiry job scheduled after commit. |

## STRIDE Threat Register
| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-12 | Tampering | client-supplied price / delivery fee / QR amount | high | mitigate | Price resolved from prices table (Phase-1 rule); fee recomputed via computeDeliveryFee; QR amount = server total; no client money field accepted. |
| T-02-13 | Tampering | choosing a freshness-disallowed method | high | mitigate | Re-enforce allowedMethodsForCart at checkout → 422, not only in the quote. |
| T-02-14 | Denial of Service | order commits but process dies before boss.send → stranded reserved stock | medium | mitigate | holdExpiresAt snapshotted; 02-07 periodic sweep is the authoritative self-heal (Pitfall 2). |
| T-02-15 | Repudiation | disputed consent | medium | mitigate | consent_logs stores usage+marketing with policyVersion + timestamp per order (PLAT-04). |
| T-02-16 | Tampering | QR amount = subtotal only, under-collects delivery | high | mitigate | QR amount = subtotal + deliveryFee (Pitfall 5), verified against the same total downstream. |
</threat_model>
